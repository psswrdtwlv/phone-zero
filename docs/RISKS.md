# PHONE ZERO — RISK REGISTER

Initial high-level risks:

1. Application ecosystem / essential apps.
2. Custom kernel may consume capacity without sufficient product advantage.
3. Virtual results may fail to transfer to physical hardware.
4. Low-volume BOM may invalidate target retail economics.
5. Camera / ISP / GPU integration complexity.
6. Cellular modem, RF and certification complexity.
7. Battery, power management and thermal engineering.
8. Manufacturing and support economics.
9. Security architecture and secure boot.
10. One-founder + AI execution capacity.

These are starting risks, not final conclusions.
Genesis and adversarial review must refine them.
